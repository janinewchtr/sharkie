How to Play

Fin Slap Puffer Fish

Your Mission

Puffer Fish Defeat them with Sharkie's fin slap.
Jellyfish Defeat them with normal bubbles.
Endboss Collect at least 5 poison bottles before reaching the endboss. Near the endboss, Sharkie automatically shoots poison bubbles instead of normal bubbles. Hit the endboss with 5 poison bubbles to defeat him.

If you run out of poison before defeating the endboss, the game is over.
